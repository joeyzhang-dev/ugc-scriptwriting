# Four things you should not be doing — gossip / content / dating / prayer

## As written

TEXT HOOK: 4 things you should not be doing if you're a "Christian"
HOOK: Here are four things you should not be doing if you claim to be a Christian.

Number one is gossip. Ephesians 4:29 says do not let any unwholesome talk come out of your mouths. If you're only saying it because they're not in the room, that's gossip.

Number two is consuming content. The shows, the music, the scrolling. Psalm 101:3 says I will set no worthless thing before my eyes. You can't feed your mind garbage all day and wonder why your prayers feel dry.

Number four is only talking to God when you need something. And I was so guilty of this. 1 Thessalonians 5:17 says pray without ceasing.
