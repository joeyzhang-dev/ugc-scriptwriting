# Christian — hook bank

> Hooks that landed for this niche, grouped by pattern. Added automatically when
> a hook works during a session.
> **Status: v1, seeded from 2 scripts.**

Two hooks ship per script: a **TEXT HOOK** (on-screen) and a spoken **HOOK**.
They are not the same line, and the difference is deliberate.

## Count + identity challenge

- "There are four things you should not be doing if you claim to be a Christian." — format: Things not to do — source: mine — **200k+ views**
- "Here are four things you should not be doing if you claim to be a Christian." — format: Things not to do — source: mine — 20-30k views (as written)
- "Here are four things you should not be doing if you're a Christian." — format: Things not to do — source: mine — 20-30k views (as delivered)

Observations across the three:

- **"There are" opened the winner; "Here are" opened the weaker one.** One data
  point each — not yet a rule.
- **"claim to be a Christian" is the stronger framing** than "you're a
  Christian". It challenges identity instead of describing it. The winner used
  it; the weaker video softened it in delivery even though the written script had
  it.

## On-screen text hooks

- `4 things you should not be doing if you're a "Christian"` — format: Things not to do — source: mine — 20-30k views

The scare quotes around "Christian" carry the challenge visually. The spoken hook
delivers the same challenge with the words "claim to be" instead.
